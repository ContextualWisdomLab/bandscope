# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

BandScope maintainers and contributors commit to a respectful, harassment-free project for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- be respectful and professional
- assume good intent and give actionable feedback
- focus criticism on ideas and code, not people
- respect community safety, privacy, and security norms

## Unacceptable behavior

- harassment, hate speech, or personal attacks
- doxxing or sharing private information without consent
- threats, intimidation, or deliberate disruption
- pressuring maintainers to bypass review, security, or governance controls

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when this code of conduct is violated.

## Reporting

Use the repository security and governance contacts documented in `SECURITY.md` and `docs/repository/governance.md`.
